# Mathematics

## Algebra

A variable is a symbol representing an unknown value. An equation states two expressions are equal. To solve a linear equation, isolate the variable using inverse operations. The quadratic formula solves ax² + bx + c = 0: x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a. The discriminant b²−4ac determines the number of real solutions: positive = two, zero = one, negative = none.

Functions map each input to exactly one output. Function notation f(x) reads "f of x." The domain is the set of valid inputs; the range is the set of outputs. A linear function has the form f(x) = mx + b where m is slope and b is y-intercept.

Systems of equations can be solved by substitution, elimination, or graphing. A consistent system has at least one solution. An inconsistent system has no solution (parallel lines). A dependent system has infinitely many solutions (same line).

## Geometry

A point has no dimension. A line extends infinitely in both directions. A line segment has two endpoints. An angle is formed by two rays from a common vertex. A right angle measures 90°. Supplementary angles sum to 180°. Complementary angles sum to 90°.

Triangles: the sum of interior angles is always 180°. An equilateral triangle has three equal sides and angles of 60°. A right triangle satisfies the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c² where c is the hypotenuse.

Area formulas: rectangle = length × width, triangle = ½ × base × height, circle = πr², trapezoid = ½(b₁ + b₂) × h. Circumference of a circle = 2πr. Volume of a sphere = (4/3)πr³. Volume of a cylinder = πr²h.

Congruent figures have the same size and shape. Similar figures have the same shape but different sizes. Corresponding angles are equal in similar figures and sides are proportional.

## Trigonometry

In a right triangle, sine = opposite/hypotenuse, cosine = adjacent/hypotenuse, tangent = opposite/adjacent. The mnemonic SOH-CAH-TOA helps remember these ratios.

The unit circle has radius 1 centered at the origin. Angles are measured in radians: 2π radians = 360°. Common values: sin(30°) = 0.5, sin(45°) = √2/2, sin(60°) = √3/2. The Pythagorean identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

## Calculus

A limit describes the value a function approaches as the input approaches a given value. Derivatives measure the rate of change of a function. The derivative of f(x) = xⁿ is f'(x) = nxⁿ⁻¹ (power rule). The chain rule: d/dx[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) · g'(x).

Integration is the reverse of differentiation. The definite integral ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx gives the area under a curve from a to b. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects derivatives and integrals: ∫ₐᵇ f'(x)dx = f(b) − f(a).

## Statistics and Probability

Mean is the arithmetic average. Median is the middle value. Mode is the most frequent value. Range is the difference between maximum and minimum. Standard deviation measures how spread out values are from the mean.

Probability of an event = number of favorable outcomes / total outcomes. Probabilities range from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). Independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B). Mutually exclusive events: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

A normal distribution is bell-shaped and symmetric about the mean. About 68% of data falls within one standard deviation, 95% within two, and 99.7% within three (the empirical rule).

## Number Theory

A prime number has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. A composite number has more than two factors. Every composite number can be expressed as a unique product of primes (Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic). The greatest common factor (GCF) is the largest number dividing two integers. The least common multiple (LCM) is the smallest positive multiple of both.

Integers include positive whole numbers, zero, and negative whole numbers. Rational numbers can be expressed as fractions p/q where q ≠ 0. Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as fractions (e.g., π, √2). Real numbers include all rational and irrational numbers.
